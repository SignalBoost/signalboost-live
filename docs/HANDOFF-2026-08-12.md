## Self-Healing Supervisor monitoring correction

The product should be described as proactive and monitor-capable, not merely reactive to alerts from another product. Native monitoring is default-capable; buyer monitoring may coexist in hybrid mode or replace it by explicit buyer choice.

## Safety/automation doctrine

Routine remediation may be automatic only when it is explicitly registered and pre-authorized within bounded capability/resource/method/parameter/reversibility/execution limits. Consequential, destructive, financial, credential/security, and otherwise approval-required operations continue through the signed approval path. Monitoring itself is observation-only and does not create mutation authority.
