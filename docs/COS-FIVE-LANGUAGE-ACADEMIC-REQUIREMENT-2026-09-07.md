# COS Five-Language Academic Requirement

**Date:** 2026-09-07  
**Status:** normative supplement to `SKILLS.md` for SignalBoost language education  
**Applies to:** COS generalist education and relevant COS specialists/assistants

## Requirement

SignalBoost's five first-class platform languages are:

1. English (`en`)
2. Spanish (`es`)
3. Portuguese (`pt`)
4. Polish (`pl`)
5. Russian (`ru`)

The `Language & Communication` subject in `SKILLS.md` must therefore not be treated as one averaged multilingual score. Each of the five platform languages is an independently measured competency.

A high result in English cannot compensate for weak Polish, Russian, Spanish, or Portuguese. Likewise, a high translation result cannot hide weak instruction-following or writing in the same language.

## Required competency dimensions per language

Each platform language must be capable of independent assessment in at least:

- comprehension;
- writing;
- instruction following;
- translation and localization;
- cultural/pragmatic communication.

The same evidence discipline used by COS University applies: corpus size, translated string counts, self-reported fluency, or exposure to material do not establish competence.

## Academic evidence

A university language grade must come from fresh, independent, host-controlled assessments. Stronger grades require stronger evidence classes, including unseen work and transfer rather than memorized translations or repeated fixtures.

For platform-language graduation readiness, every required language must independently satisfy the configured target grade. Do not average the five languages into a passing mean.

## Continuous learning

Language education continues after qualification. COS should detect and remediate weaknesses caused by:

- grammar or vocabulary gaps;
- unnatural or non-native phrasing;
- instruction misunderstanding;
- translation/localization errors;
- cultural or pragmatic mistakes;
- stale terminology or changing usage;
- domain-specific vocabulary gaps.

Study methods may vary by gap: owner-fed material, books, corpora, live authoritative/current sources, native-language examples, teacher/tutor agents, peer-agent interaction, translation exercises, conversation simulations, writing practice, production feedback, and independent unseen exams.

## Relationship to the platform

The requirement is broader than UI localization. COS should be able to reason and communicate effectively in the five languages when the task requires it, while preserving evidence, factual accuracy, governance, and the owner's intent across languages.

**Canonical rule:** EN / ES / PT / PL / RU are five separate required language competencies. No language or skill dimension may be averaged away.
