SignalBoost Concierge native-language quality gate
Date: 2026-09-07

Supported languages: English, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, Russian.

Acceptance rules:
1. Selected UI language reaches the Concierge/COS request context.
2. Model-backed answers are authored directly in the selected language using native grammar, idiom, register, and terminology.
3. Polish specifically requires correct case, verb aspect, gender/number agreement, natural word order, and consistent Pan/Pani versus ty register.
4. Deterministic fallback paths are native in all five languages and may not leak English boilerplate.
5. Multilingual intent recognition must work from native-language requests.
6. Language-only corrections may not alter facts, numbers, URLs, citations, code, product identifiers, or governance/safety boundaries.
7. Contextual interpretation must remain before mature retrieval.
