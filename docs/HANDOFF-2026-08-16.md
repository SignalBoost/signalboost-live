## Business Intelligence Corpus conservative promotion rules

`POST /api/admin/business-intelligence-corpus/seed-prospect-history` remains the authoritative validated-history seeder.

Key rules:

- dry-run by default; apply requires explicit `apply: true`
- maximum bounded apply limit 500
- zero external provider calls
- zero external AI calls
- registrable commercial root domains only
- rejects government/military/education/noncommercial roots and tracking hosts
- company identity must match root-domain identity
- identity evidence requires at least two independent campaign jobs OR exact same-domain drafted-contact evidence
- ready promotion additionally requires reusable stored description OR exact same-domain contact evidence
- repeated identity alone is not corpus-ready
- preserve richer existing canonical/fallback records; never overwrite them with weaker history

Earlier analysis found 82 conservative identity-valid historical organizations and about 24 with reusable descriptions, but those figures are analysis, not a persisted production increment. Re-run the owner dry-run before quoting an exact current eligible set.
